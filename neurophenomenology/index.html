Neurophenomenology

lived experience as data
VIDEOS COMING SOON 20 CHAPTERS + BONUS

A method guide for bringing lived experience into a study, from enaction and the first-, second- and third-person views to interviews, scales, coding, statistics and the link with brain signals. Each tool is shown with what it produces, what it cannot say and what to report, following one experience: meeting someone for the first time.
