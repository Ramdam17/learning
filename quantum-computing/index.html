Quantum computing

linear algebra, not magic
84 VIDEOS 16 CHAPTERS + BONUS

From complex numbers and the qubit to Shor, Grover, error correction and quantum machine learning: quantum computing as linear algebra on complex vectors, read out by measurement. Every speed-up is set against the best classical method, so claims of quantum advantage can be judged. QUBY, ORA, FIZZ and ABACUS guide the series.
