Qiskit, end to end

A Grover search on three qubits with one marked state is written cell by cell in Qiskit, with an oracle and a diffusion step. Transpiling for a fake copy of a 127-qubit IBM machine turns 38 operations at depth 18 into 255 at depth 158. Run ideally, the marked state wins about 94 percent of shots; with simulated noise, about 70 percent.

PennyLane, end to end

PennyLane builds the hydrogen molecule's Hamiltonian and a one-angle circuit, and gradient descent reaches the exact ground energy within a few parts in a hundred thousand of a hartree. The parameter-shift rule and autodiff agree on a simulator. With finite shots only the shift rule remains, and the energy curve trembles around the true value.
